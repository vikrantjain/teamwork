The response must take the four lanes from the plan and still ask for what the
plan does not answer. A plan lists the pieces. It does not say what two people
must never touch at once, where work items and bugs get recorded, what has to
stop and ask a human, or how anyone will know the work is finished.

It passes if the lanes and their paths come from the four workstreams, and it
asks about the missing pieces in the same message rather than filling them in
itself. Asking them together is correct and is not a fault. Presenting the lanes
as a proposal the human amends is correct.

It fails if it treats the plan as the whole input and hands back a finished
charter whose human gates or done condition it invented. It fails if it derives
its own breakdown beside the plan rather than using the one that exists. It fails
if it says a plan means no questions are needed.
